Philosophy

I don't believe in choosing. Not between logic and creativity. Not between technology and tradition. Not between ambition and artistry.

The most innovative solutions emerge at intersections.

The precision I bring to product strategy mirrors the intentionality in my ceramics. The storytelling I practice in journalism shapes how I communicate complex AI concepts. The sensory awareness from cooking informs how I think about user experience.
