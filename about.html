About

The Story

I never fit into one box, and I stopped trying to. While others were choosing between tech and the arts, between logic and creativity, between building products and building sculptures, I was asking: why not all of it?

My days are spent shaping the future of AI—translating complex algorithms into experiences that feel intuitive, human, alive. I lead product teams, define strategy, and ensure that as we build more powerful technology, we never lose sight of the people it serves.

"The ceramics studio teaches me what the boardroom can't: patience, imperfection, the beauty of things made by hand."

But when I leave the office, I return to clay. To words. To the kitchen. Each practice teaches me something the others can't. The precision of product management mirrors the intentionality of plating a dish. The narrative arc I learned in journalism shapes how I communicate vision to stakeholders. The tactile, meditative practice of ceramics grounds me in a world that's increasingly digital.

This isn't about being "well-rounded." It's about recognizing that innovation happens at intersections. The most compelling solutions emerge when you can see problems from multiple angles, when you understand both systems and stories, when you value both data and intuition.
